Things to try

  • Launch Mode: hold brake and throttle together on the line, release on green. Skip it and the torque ramps in — you’ll lose about a tenth.
  • One-pedal driving: lift off and regen slows you at ~0.2 g, putting energy back in the pack. Watch the green side of the power bar.
  • Driver seat cam (C): roof off, wind noise up — the best way to feel the shove.
  • G-meter: on a perfect launch you’ll see roughly 1.4 g. With the thruster pack, more.
  • Autopilot ride-along (T): let the car drive and just watch the numbers.
