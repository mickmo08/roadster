What's modelled

  • Multi-mode suction fans: about 1.1 g of suction, even when stopped. That's why the car can hang upside down on the reveal ramp and drive the loop slowly.
  • Cold-gas thrusters: finite gas (watch the COPV gauge), recharged by the onboard compressor or instantly on the blue recharge pad.
  • Rocket flight assist: hold Space with no stick input and the car self-levels like a drone. Combine with Q/E and W/S for rolls and flips.
  • Active rear wing: deploys at speed, under braking and in the air. The doors are butterfly-style; open them in the menu showroom.
  • Landings: land on your wheels, under about 16 m/s vertical, within 45° of level. Anything else is a rapid unscheduled disassembly.
